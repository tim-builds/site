Delete your OpenHoops account

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, check-ins, thumbs-ups ("daps"), "heading there" plans, favorite courts, reports, blocks, and notification token. There is no undo.

Fastest: in the app

Open OpenHoops → Profile tab → gear icon (top right) → scroll to the bottom → Delete account → confirm twice.

Deletion is immediate.

Without the app: by email

Email support@timbuilds.dev with the subject "Delete my OpenHoops account", sent from the email address you signed up with (that's how we verify it's you). We will delete the account and confirm within 30 days.

What is deleted, and when

All data associated with your account is removed at deletion time: account email, username, optional name and photo, check-in history, daps given and received, plans, favorites, reports and blocks, and your notification token. Nothing is retained afterward except records we are legally required to keep (we don't expect any for a basketball app).
