OpenHoops Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026 · Contact: flushatoilet@gmail.com

OpenHoops ("the app") helps basketball players find gyms and outdoor courts and see who is playing right now. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The short version: we collect the minimum needed to run the app, we don't sell anything, and your location is never tracked in the background.

What we collect

Account information. When you sign up we collect your email address and a username you choose. Your email is used for signing in and account recovery. Other players see only your username — never your email.

Optional profile details. You may add your first and last name and a profile picture. These are entirely optional. Your name is hidden by default — other players see it only if you choose a display option in settings (initials, first name only, or first name plus last initial; your full last name is never displayed to anyone). You can switch back to username-only at any time. Profile pictures are stored on our infrastructure provider and are visible to other players. Leaving these blank changes nothing about how the app works.

Location (while using the app). The app uses your device's precise location for two purposes only: showing venues near you on the map, and verifying that you are physically at a venue when you check in (within about 50 meters of an outdoor court, or 200 meters of a gym — gyms use a wider ring because GPS is less accurate inside large buildings). Location is read only while the app is open. Check-in verification happens at the moment you check in; the app does not keep a history of your movements or track you in the background. Automatic check-in works by recording a brief pending arrival when the open app sees you at a venue; it becomes a check-in about a minute later unless you leave first, and can be turned off at any time in settings.

Check-ins and thumbs-ups. When you check in, we store which venue you checked in at and when; other players can see your active check-in and its arrival time on that venue's roster. Check-ins expire automatically after 3 hours. When you give another player a thumbs-up ("daps"), it is stored and shown to the recipient anonymously — the app never reveals who gave a thumbs-up.

"Heading there" plans. If you announce you're heading to a court at a certain time, we store that plan (venue and estimated arrival) and show it to other players with your username. Plans disappear automatically once the time passes, and you can cancel one at any time.

Favorite courts. Courts you star are stored so the app can alert you when players show up there. Your favorites list is visible only to you.

Notification token. If you allow notifications in the store version of the app, your device's push-notification token (a random address for delivering notifications — not your phone number or any hardware ID) is stored so alerts about your favorite courts can reach your device with the app closed. It is removed when your account is deleted.

Reports and blocks. If you report a player, we store the report (who you reported, the category, and where) so we can review it; reports are never shown to the reported player. If you block a player, that is stored so the app can hide the two of you from each other.

What we don't collect

No contacts, no photos, no files, no payment information, no health data, no advertising identifiers. This version of the app contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK.

Where your data lives

App data is stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider), hosted in the United States (AWS us-east-2). All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules ensure players can only read the information the app is designed to show.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Data is processed only by our infrastructure provider (Supabase) as required to operate the app.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. You can delete it three ways:

  1. In the app: Profile → gear icon → Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, check-ins, thumbs-ups, plans, favorites, reports, blocks, and notification token.
  2. On the web: see our account-deletion page — no app required.
  3. By email: write to flushatoilet@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we will complete the deletion within 30 days.

Children

OpenHoops is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update this page and the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means the new policy applies.
